Hermes Agent — Personal Google Account Integration

A private, single-user integration. Not a public product and not offered to third parties.

What this application is

This is a personal automation integration between the owner's own installation of Hermes Agent (a locally run AI assistant) and the owner's own Google account. The application is registered as a Google Cloud OAuth client created by the account owner for their sole use.

There is exactly one user: the owner of this Google account. The application is not distributed, not published in any app store, has no sign-up, and cannot be authorized by anyone other than the account owner.

What it does

When the owner asks the assistant a question or gives it an instruction from their own computer, the assistant may perform the following operations on the owner's Google account, on demand and only when requested:

  • Gmail — search and read messages, and send or reply to email that the owner has composed or approved.
  • Google Calendar — list, create and delete calendar events.
  • Google Drive — search, read, upload, download, organize and delete files.
  • Google Sheets — read and write spreadsheet values.
  • Google Docs — read documents and create or append text.
  • Google Contacts — read the owner's contact list (read-only).

Every action is initiated interactively by the owner. No automated bulk processing, no background harvesting of data, and no data is collected from any account other than the owner's own.

How data is handled

All processing happens locally on the owner's own computer. There is no backend server, no database, and no third-party service in the middle. Credentials, tokens and any retrieved data stay on the owner's device. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.
